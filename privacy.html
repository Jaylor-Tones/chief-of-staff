Privacy policy

Effective 2026-11-17

Chief of staff is a personal assistant app. It runs on its owner's own computer and connects only to the owner's own Google accounts. It isn't offered to the public. This policy explains what Google data it uses and how.

What it accesses

  • Gmail: messages, threads, labels and drafts, so it can search and read mail, organize it, save drafts, and send mail.
  • Google Calendar: calendars, events and free/busy times, so it can read the schedule and add, change or remove events.

How it uses that data

Only to do what the owner asks, such as summarizing what needs attention, drafting replies, sending a message the owner asked for, or scheduling an event. It doesn't use Google data for advertising, and it doesn't use it to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

Where it's processed and stored

  • The app runs on the owner's computer. Google sign-in tokens are stored there, readable only by the owner's user account.
  • To carry out a request, the content that request needs (for example, the emails being summarized) is processed by the AI assistant the app runs on, Anthropic's Claude.
  • The app keeps only what the owner asks it to keep, such as notes and drafts, plus a local log of the emails it has sent. These stay on the owner's computer.

Sharing

Google data isn't sold, rented or shared with anyone, apart from the processing described above that is needed to carry out the owner's requests.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Chief of staff's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

You can remove Chief of staff's access to a Google account at any time on your Google Account's third-party connections page. Deleting the app's folder from the owner's computer deletes everything it stored locally.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

For questions about this policy, open an issue on GitHub.
