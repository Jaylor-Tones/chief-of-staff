Chief of staff

A personal assistant app that helps its owner keep on top of their own email and calendar.

What it does

  • Reads and searches the owner's Gmail to show what needs their attention.
  • Saves draft replies in Gmail for the owner to review.
  • Sends an email only when the owner asks for that message.
  • Reads the owner's Google Calendar, checks when they're free, and adds or moves events they ask for.

Who it's for

Chief of staff is a private tool. It runs on its owner's own computer and connects only to the owner's own Google accounts. It isn't offered to the public, and there's no sign-up.

Your data

Chief of staff uses Google data only to do what its owner asks. It doesn't sell it, show ads, or use it to train AI models. The privacy policy explains exactly what it accesses and how.
